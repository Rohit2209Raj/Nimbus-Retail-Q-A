# Nimbus Retail Pvt Ltd — Information Security Guidelines

## 1. Purpose
These guidelines define minimum security practices expected from every employee to protect company, customer, and vendor data.

## 2. Device and Access Management
All employees must use company-issued devices for accessing internal systems, including email, HR tools, and customer databases. Personal laptops or phones may only be used to access company email through the approved mobile app with mandatory app-level PIN lock enabled. VPN connectivity is mandatory when accessing internal applications from any network outside the corporate office, including home Wi-Fi.

## 3. Password Policy
Passwords for all company systems must be at least 12 characters long, include a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols, and must be changed every 90 days. Reuse of the last 5 passwords is not permitted. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all systems handling customer or financial data.

## 4. Data Classification
Data is classified into three tiers: Public (marketing material), Internal (org charts, internal memos), and Confidential (customer PII, financial records, source code). Confidential data must never be shared over personal email, WhatsApp, or unapproved cloud storage such as personal Google Drive or Dropbox accounts.

## 5. Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident — phishing email, lost device, unauthorized access, suspicious login alert — must be reported to the Security team within 1 hour of discovery via the dedicated Slack channel #security-incidents or email security@nimbusretail.example. Delayed reporting beyond 24 hours must be documented with a reason during the post-incident review.

## 6. Third-Party Tools and AI Assistants
Employees must not paste confidential customer data, source code, or financial figures into any external AI tool, chatbot, or SaaS product that has not been explicitly approved by the Security team. A list of approved tools is maintained on the internal Security Wiki.

## 7. Offboarding
Upon resignation or termination, all company devices, access badges, and credentials must be returned or revoked on the last working day. Access to internal systems is automatically disabled at 11:59 PM on the last working day as recorded in the HR system.

## 8. Physical Security
Visitors to any Nimbus Retail office must be signed in at reception and accompanied by an employee at all times. Employees must not hold the office door open for unrecognized individuals ("tailgating") without verifying their identity at reception.
