# Nimbus Retail Pvt Ltd — New Employee Onboarding Guide

## 1. Before Day 1
HR will share your offer confirmation, background verification form, and a checklist of documents required (ID proof, address proof, previous employment relieving letter, educational certificates). These must be submitted digitally at least 3 working days before your joining date.

## 2. Day 1 Checklist
- Report to the reception at 9:30 AM for badge issuance and orientation.
- IT will hand over your company laptop and set up your official email ID (firstname.lastname@nimbusretail.example).
- Complete mandatory e-learning modules: Code of Conduct, Information Security Guidelines, and POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) training, all within the first 5 working days.
- Meet your reporting manager and assigned buddy (a peer from your team who helps with initial questions).

## 3. Probation Period
All new employees undergo a probation period of 3 months from the date of joining, extendable by up to 3 additional months at the manager's discretion if performance criteria are not fully met. Confirmation of employment is communicated in writing by HR after successful completion of probation. Leave entitlements under the Leave Policy begin accruing from Day 1, but Casual Leave and Earned Leave cannot be availed until probation is confirmed, except in emergencies with HR approval.

## 4. Systems Access
Access to internal tools (HR portal, Expense Portal, Slack, project management tools) is provisioned within the first 48 hours. Access to customer-facing systems or financial systems requires additional manager-level approval and typically takes 3-5 working days.

## 5. First 30 Days
New employees are expected to complete a structured 30-60-90 day plan created jointly with their manager, covering initial learning goals, small deliverables, and integration with the team. A check-in with HR is scheduled at the end of the first month to gather feedback on the onboarding experience.
