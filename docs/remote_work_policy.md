# Nimbus Retail Pvt Ltd — Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## 1. Eligibility
Employees in roles classified as "Remote-Eligible" by their department head may work remotely for up to 3 days per week. Roles involving warehouse operations, in-store retail support, or physical inventory management are not eligible for remote work.

## 2. Core Working Hours
All remote employees must be available and responsive between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM IST on working days, regardless of their chosen work location. Meetings scheduled within this window take priority over personal commitments unless pre-communicated.

## 3. Equipment and Reimbursement
The company provides a one-time home-office setup allowance of INR 15,000 for employees newly approved for remote work, covering items such as a monitor, chair, or desk. Ongoing internet/phone reimbursement is covered separately under the Expense Reimbursement Policy (Section 2.4).

## 4. Reporting Illness While Working Remotely
If a remote employee is unwell and unable to work, they must inform their reporting manager via email or the team chat channel before 10:00 AM on that day. This is logged as sick leave under the standard Leave Policy and follows the same medical certificate requirement for absences exceeding 2 consecutive days.

## 5. Data Security for Remote Work
Remote employees must use only company-issued laptops for accessing internal systems. Personal devices may not be used to access customer data, financial systems, or internal HR tools under any circumstance. VPN must be active at all times when accessing internal applications from outside the office network.

## 6. Attendance Tracking
Remote work days must be marked in advance on the internal Workday-style attendance tool. Employees working from a location other than their registered home address for more than 5 consecutive days must inform HR in advance.

## 7. Review and Revocation
Remote work privileges are reviewed quarterly based on performance and availability during core hours. Repeated unavailability during core hours without prior notice may result in revocation of remote work eligibility by the reporting manager.
