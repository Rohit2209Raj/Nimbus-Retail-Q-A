# Nimbus Retail Pvt Ltd — Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy governs how employees can claim reimbursement for business-related expenses incurred while performing their duties.

## 2. Eligible Expense Categories

### 2.1 Travel Expenses
Domestic travel for business purposes is reimbursed at actuals for economy class flights and AC 2-tier train fare. Local commute during official travel (cabs, autos) is reimbursed up to INR 1,500 per day with receipts. Employees below the Manager level require pre-approval from their reporting manager before booking any travel exceeding INR 10,000.

### 2.2 Accommodation
Hotel stays during business travel are reimbursed up to INR 4,500 per night in metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune) and INR 3,000 per night in other cities. Bookings must be made through the approved corporate travel portal wherever possible.

### 2.3 Meals
A daily meal allowance of INR 800 is provided during business travel, claimable without individual receipts up to this limit. Amounts exceeding this limit require itemized bills.

### 2.4 Internet and Phone (Remote/Field Employees)
Employees working remotely or in field roles can claim up to INR 1,000 per month towards internet and mobile bills, submitted quarterly with proof of payment.

### 2.5 Client Entertainment
Client entertainment expenses require prior written approval from a Director-level employee and must be submitted with itemized bills and the client's name and company within 7 days of the expense.

## 3. Submission Process
All reimbursement claims must be filed through the Expense Portal within 15 days of incurring the expense, along with scanned copies of original receipts. Claims submitted after 30 days will not be processed except under exceptional circumstances approved by Finance.

## 4. Approval Workflow
Claims under INR 5,000 are auto-approved upon manager sign-off. Claims between INR 5,000 and INR 25,000 require Finance review in addition to manager approval. Claims above INR 25,000 require Director approval.

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Items
The following are explicitly not reimbursable: alcoholic beverages, personal entertainment, traffic fines or penalties, upgrades to business/first class without prior written approval, and expenses for family members accompanying the employee on business travel.

## 6. Reimbursement Timeline
Approved claims are processed and credited to the employee's registered bank account within 10 working days of final approval.
