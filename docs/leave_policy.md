# Nimbus Retail Pvt Ltd — Leave Policy

## 1. Overview
This document describes the leave entitlements available to all full-time employees of Nimbus Retail Pvt Ltd, effective from the date of joining after successful completion of the probation period.

## 2. Types of Leave

### 2.1 Casual Leave (CL)
Employees are entitled to 12 days of casual leave per calendar year, credited at the rate of 1 day per month. Casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next year and lapses on December 31st.

### 2.2 Sick Leave (SL)
Employees receive 8 days of sick leave per calendar year. For sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate from a registered practitioner must be submitted to HR within 3 working days of resuming work. Sick leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 15 days.

### 2.3 Earned Leave (EL)
Earned leave accrues at 1.5 days per completed month of service (18 days/year). Earned leave must be applied for at least 5 working days in advance through the HR portal and requires manager approval. Unused earned leave can be encashed at the end of the financial year, capped at 30 days.

### 2.4 Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is granted as per the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 (26 weeks for the first two children). Paternity leave is 15 calendar days, to be availed within 3 months of the child's birth.

### 2.5 Bereavement Leave
5 days of paid bereavement leave is granted in case of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, parent, child, or sibling).

## 3. Leave Application Process
All leave requests, except emergency sick leave, must be submitted through the HR portal at least 24 hours in advance. Emergency sick leave should be reported to the reporting manager via email or phone before the start of the working day, followed by a formal application on the portal within 2 working days.

## 4. Leave During Notice Period
Employees serving their notice period are not entitled to earned leave encashment beyond what has already accrued as of the last working day. Casual and sick leave cannot be availed during the notice period without prior written approval from HR.

## 5. Public Holidays
Nimbus Retail observes 12 public holidays annually, as per the list published on the HR portal every January. Employees required to work on a public holiday due to business needs are entitled to a compensatory day off within 30 days.
